## Tensions Identified

1. **Fixed vs Plastic**
   - Frozen shape should be fixed (the "character")
   - Topology should be plastic (the "learning")
   - Where's the boundary?

2. **Local vs Global**
   - Frustration is local
   - Identity is global (the whole topology)
   - How does local reshape global?

3. **Speed vs Stability**
   - Fast rewiring = rapid learning
   - Fast rewiring = unstable identity
   - How to balance?

4. **Growth vs Computation**
   - Growing the topology takes time
   - Computing with fixed topology is fast
   - When to grow, when to compute?

5. **Self vs Other**
   - The topology is the self
   - The input is the other
   - When does input become self? (Integration)
